What broke, what it stopped, and the way to fix it.

The hub is the page that shows every channel at once — the one place you would notice a channel had gone quiet. It is the only one that cannot tell you why.

03Three ways out

Every fix is really an answer to one question: is the hub a signpost, or is it the workbench?

Option A

The hub becomes a signpost

Hub→ Channel page

Pick a channel and it takes you to that channel's page. The hub stops showing lists altogether. One page to maintain, and the warning comes for free.

Conflicts with a call you already made. You asked for Returns to open inside the hub “without teleporting”. This brings the teleporting back.
Option B

The hub becomes the only workbench

Hub   Channel page

The channel's own orders page goes away and everything happens in the hub. Matches what you asked for, and there is only ever one screen.

The biggest job of the three. Those channel pages also carry products, settings and returns, so they cannot simply be deleted — each one needs somewhere to go first.
Option C — recommended

Both stay, and share one frame

Hub Channel page ↓ one shared frame

Keep both pages, but make the warning region, the trail and the title come from one place instead of two. The hub gains the banner immediately.

Smallest change, fixes the real harm. Nothing moves for you. It also stops the next bug from having to be fixed twice.

What I'd do

Option C now, Option B later — if ever. The thing that actually hurts is the missing warning on the page where you would notice a channel had gone quiet. C fixes that without moving anything you use, and it can be done and checked in one sitting.

B is the tidier end state and it matches how you said you want to work, but it is a project: the channel pages hold more than orders, so each of those screens needs a home before the page can go. That is worth planning deliberately, not as a side effect of fixing a banner.

A I would not do. It undoes a decision you already made for a good reason.
