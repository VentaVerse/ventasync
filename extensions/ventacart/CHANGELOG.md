# VentaCart changelog

## 0.31.1 (2026-11-02)

### New features

- Edit listing can fill its fields from a listing template, and nothing is saved until Save listing.

### Updates

- Sync stock and Sync price are back on each listing's menu and on the selection bar.
- A product group no longer holds a listing template; a template is applied from the Products page selection bar.
- Its CSS and JavaScript now ship inside the extension and load only while it is enabled.

## 0.31.0 (2026-10-23)

### New features

- Listing templates, and Apply template on the Products page with a preview.
- A variation can have its own price on this store, beside the Master Catalog price.
- Product groups have their own page, with their products and a listing template.
- Phone app: order lines show the photo of the variation that was ordered.

### Updates

- A send reads only the listing; product groups are filters with no settings of their own.
- A product's brand comes from the Master Catalog; the store's brand list is no longer fetched.
- On a hosted server, store addresses must be public https addresses. Self-hosted installs are unchanged.
- Some minor improvements.

## 0.30.0 (2026-10-07)

- First numbered release, opening the closed beta.
